# Decisions log

Where the sources disagreed, these are the calls made and why. Each is a default; a site's plan may override with a reason recorded in that repo's `docs/patterns/`. Decided 2026-10-24 on Andrew's instruction to go with the recommendations in the structure analysis.

| # | Decision | Chosen | Against | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-A | Service slugs on a single-city site | Bare slug, city in title and H1 | `-in-<city>` (dhennessy, earlier patterns doc, checker `S-slug-geo`) | All three group sites build bare slugs; the city carries more weight in title, H1 and schema than in a folder name. `S-slug-geo` becomes per-site, default off |
| D-B | Location URLs | `/<trade-phrase>-<city>/<town>/`, hub as parent | `/locations/<place>/` | The URL says the search; towns inherit their hub |
| D-C | Location anchors | Hubs: the target phrase ("Roofers Cork"). Towns: bare name in chip lists, "{Trade phrase} in {Town}" on hub town cards, "Roofers {Town}" in prose and article chips | Bare place everywhere (dhennessy) | Matches what ranks on RR (768 hub anchors) and keeps town chips readable |
| D-D | Article-to-article links | Allowed, structured: spoke → pillar once, pillar → spokes, Read-next line, nearby and other-cities section. No open mesh; no post links from towns or services | None in prose (draft, patterns doc) | Patric's method requires them; dhennessy's winning post links siblings; the widget-only rule was already the shipped position |
| D-D2 | Related reading on articles | A 3-post block scored by shared place and topic words, in addition to the structured links | Newest-first or uncapped widgets | Munster adopted it on 12 Oct 2026; Dublin Gutter's newest-first block concentrates links on the 3 newest posts |
| D-T | Inline scripts | Our builds: none (strict security header). Patric's sites use `'unsafe-inline'`: a known difference, not a rule change | none | The strict header caught the July breakage on Manchester; the loose one did not stop Munster's pixel and widgets failing silently |
| D-U | Hidden text, claims typed into templates, required review slots | Forbidden (hidden keyword headings: see D-V) | Present on Munster and Dublin Gutter | Spam policy and the accuracy rules |
| D-V | Hidden keyword headings | A per-site setting, `allowHiddenHeadings` in `site-checks.config.json`, default off. On for Andrew's own sites (Andrew, 25 October 2026); off for client sites unless the client agrees knowing the risk | Munster uses them (`kwhide`) on the home page and two county hubs | Andrew rates them a useful tactic. Google's spam policy lists hidden text, so the risk is a manual action; our data cannot isolate their effect (three pages, no control). The checker lists them as warnings when allowed |
| D-E | Question H2s | Articles ≥60%; commercial pages use topic H2s plus a visible FAQ of 6-8 (towns, services) or 8-12 (hubs) | 1+ / 3+ question H2s on every page (`h2-questions-min-count`, `mandatory-faq-on-service-page`) | Zero commercial pages on three sites use question H2s; the rules should count FAQ questions on those pages |
| D-F | FAQPage schema | Emit wherever the visible FAQ matches word for word, on every page type that has one; one FAQ component | None on blog (Patric Aug 2026); none at all (Munster) | Patric's latest rule; AI assistants parse Q&A from structured data; no penalty for valid unused schema |
| D-G | Closing CTA | Form on the page at `#quote` + tel: on every page; header button tel: or `#quote` | Tel-only or a link to /contact or /quote | Patric's 2026-09-15 rule; Dublin Gutter's /quote drain is the anti-pattern |
| D-H | Town differentiation | Place brief + weather and property-type question sections; ≥300 unique words; similarity <0.30 vs siblings; landmarks "where relevant" (warn) | 500 unique words + 3-5 landmarks as a block (`location-landmark-count`) | The landmark block would fail every live town page; the research brief is the method that produces real difference |
| D-I | In-prose links on service pages | 0-2, to the homepage or a proof page, fresh anchor | 3-5 (draft, checker `minProseLinks`) | Measured 3-layer model; service pages are sinks |
| D-J | LocalBusiness and ratings | Organization anchor + Service with `areaServed` = the town; LocalBusiness only with a real address; aggregateRating only where reviews are shown on the page | LocalBusiness + rating on every town (RR practice) | Spec and Patric's newer builds agree; unsupported ratings are worse than none |
| D-K | Year in titles and slugs | No year in H1 or slug; in a title only when reviewed that year; prices always dated | Year + auto-roll (patterns doc, `geo-blog-aio-title-optimisation`) | Rolling a year on unchanged content is a false freshness claim; Patric dropped it in October. On complete (unfiltered) data for Aug-Oct 2026 the measures disagree (mean 3.3 clicks per post with a year and 4.6 without; median 2 and 0): no reliable difference, so the rule rests on honesty, not on clicks |
| D-L | Paragraphs | Average ≤25 words, none over 60; answer paragraph 40-60 | ≤20 average (checker) | Matches Patric's measured 2-4 sentences and the audit tool's 60-word cap |
| D-M | Word counts | Town 1,200-1,800; hub 2,500+; service 2,000+; article 1,000-1,800 (floor 800); entity 300-600 | 750+ flat | Built medians; length never the goal |
| D-N | Posting cadence | None; topics only with demand evidence, judged at 28 and 90 days (Munster's demand-led articles produced about half its Aug-Oct 2026 year-on-year gain) | ≥1 post per week (patterns doc) | 21 articles in four weeks earned nothing; content volume correlates negatively with rankings in the tracked set |
| D-O | Hub for the head term | Chosen per site in the plan; that page's title, H1 and URL say the search; homepage ≥3x inbound floor, 5x target | Homepage always (dhennessy) | Group sites rank service pages; both work when deliberate |
| D-P | Schema allow-list in agency-standards | Extend to BlogPosting, ItemList, ImageGallery, Place, VideoObject, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, WebSite | Current list blocks them | Built sites use them; code change in audit-content |
| D-Q | `<main>` | Required | RR and Dublin Gutter lack it (453 pages); Munster has it on all 279 | Measured extraction benefit; one-line change in Patric's generators if he adopts it |
| D-R | Tables | Price table on cost and service pages; comparison table on articles; brands in one table per page | Not specified | Patric's gate and all three sites |
| D-S | Information gain | 3 gap items on commercial pages, 5 on articles, listed in the brief | `min-info-gain-entities` deferred | Re-adopted as warn |
